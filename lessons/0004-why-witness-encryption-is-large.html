A short proof can require a huge ciphertext.

Your goal: explain what is large and what kind of reduction would help.

The proposed AADP witness encryption represents the verification condition using large matrices. It stores a cryptographic encoding of the check, not just the small message being protected. AADP means Arithmetic Affine Determinant Program. AADP paper, introduction

Witness compression

A SNARK proof

Replaces a large witness with a succinct proof. The WE construction must still encode the computation that verifies that proof.

Verifier reduction

Fewer constraints

Reduces the algebraic structure the WE ciphertext represents. Shortening the proof alone does not guarantee this.

This distinction is explicit in the AADP introduction.

One modeled candidate, as reported by the authors

≈ 338 TB

The estimate uses 14,083 variables and 14,371 multiplication constraints. It comes from a modeling script, not a complete end-to-end implementation. The construction’s security remains heuristic. PIPEs §§5.3.1–5.4

size ∝ v × (2g + 1)² × field-element size

v counts variables; g counts multiplication constraints. The representation has roughly one square matrix per variable, with side length 2g + 1. That explains the linear variable factor and squared gate factor. Reducing both counts by half gives roughly one eighth the size.

“Needs compression” is underspecified. Ask what gets smaller: the witness, the verifier, or the ciphertext representation. The slider changes verifier complexity; it makes no claim about ZIP-style file compression.

Does Bitcoin have to store hundreds of terabytes per transfer?

No such requirement follows from this estimate. The large object is the candidate WE ciphertext. In the PIPE flow, decryption and signing happen outside Bitcoin’s script execution; the spend is authorized with a standard Schnorr signature. The ordinary Shielded Bitcoin transfer envelope is a separate object. PIPEs §4

The transfer paper’s two-input/two-output example has a 610-byte envelope, including a roughly 192-byte proof. Neither figure measures the WE ciphertext. Appendix A
